A few helpful answers
Questions,
answered.
Still finding your feet? That’s a perfectly good place to start.

        

          
Do I need a detailed brief before reaching out?
No. A rough idea, a clear problem, or an open question is enough to start a useful conversation. The brief builder above can help organize your thoughts, but you can keep it simple.

          
Is Nexa a product or a design and development studio?
Nexa is an independent design and development studio. The concept projects are exploratory portfolio pieces, not products for sale or live client projects.

          
Does the form send my details?
No. The form runs entirely in your browser. When you prepare a brief, you can review, copy, or download the text yourself; no email is sent and nothing is stored or submitted to a server.

          
What happens after I get in touch?
We can start by clarifying what you need, what success might look like, and whether the project is a good fit for the studio. Any next step would be agreed together.
